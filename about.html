About Wheely Good Tutoring

Our mission

Wheely Good Tutoring is a free, student-run peer tutoring club at Wheeler. Our mission is simple: make sure no student has to struggle alone. Every Wednesday we bring students and peer tutors together so that help is always within reach.

Why we started

The club began with a familiar feeling. A student gets confused by a concept in class, the lesson moves on, and there is no personal tutor to turn to afterward. Not everyone can access private tutoring, so we built a place where that help is free and open to anyone who shows up.

How a session works

Sessions are relaxed and student-led. Come by with the assignment or topic you want to work on. We pair you with a peer tutor who knows the subject, and you work through it together at your own pace. No appointment pressure, no cost, just students helping students.
