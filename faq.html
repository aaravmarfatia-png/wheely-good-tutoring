FAQ & Contact

Is it really free?

Yes. Wheely Good Tutoring is completely free. There is never a charge to get help or to tutor.

Who can come?

Any Wheeler student is welcome, whether you need help in one subject or several.

Do I need to sign up first?

Signing up helps us match you with the right tutor, but you are always welcome to drop in on a Wednesday.

What should I bring?

Bring the assignment, notes, or materials for whatever you want to work on, and anything you have tried so far.

How are tutors chosen?

Tutors are fellow students who are confident in a subject and want to help. Sign up on the tutor form to get involved.
